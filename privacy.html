Last Updated: August 30, 2026 | Effective immediately

Welcome to LikeX SMM Panel (accessible at https://likex.in). We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we process it, and how your rights are safeguarded when using our social media marketing platform.

🛡️ Google OAuth Compliance Note: LikeX strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy. We only access basic profile information (Name and Email) necessary to securely create your account and log you in. We never read your emails, contacts, or drive files.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information required to deliver high-quality marketing services:

  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in via Google OAuth or Email OTP, we receive your email address, full name, and authentication token.
  • Order Details: Public profile/post URLs (links to your public social media profiles or videos) and order quantities submitted by you.

  • Technical Data: Basic device information, IP address, and browser cookies used solely for session security and fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your personal customer wallet & order history.
  • To automate and execute social media service requests through our verified wholesale provider network.
  • To send you important notifications regarding order completions, balance top-ups, and security updates.
  • To provide 24/7 dedicated customer care via WhatsApp (+91 9837371137) and support tickets.

3. Google User Data Policy

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In:

  • We request only openid, email, and profile scopes.
  • Your Google email and name are used solely to create your unique LikeX member account.
  • We never share, transfer, or sell your Google user data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • No humans at LikeX have access to your personal Google passwords.

4. Data Storage and Security

We use enterprise-grade encryption (TLS/HTTPS), modern cryptographic hashing for session tokens, and secure PostgreSQL infrastructure to keep your account safe from unauthorized access.

You have full control over your personal data. You may request to review, update, or permanently delete your account and associated records at any time by emailing us at supporthubindia@gmail.com. All personal identifiers will be permanently removed within 48 hours of verification.
